📖 Truth or Trickster — How to Play & Game Rules

🎯 The Premise

Truth or Trickster is a social deduction party bluffing game played with one main screen (Host) and multiple contestants connected from their smartphones.

In each round, one contestant takes the Hot Seat (Speaker) and receives a secret prompt. They are secretly assigned either to tell a 100% True Story or to fabricate a believable Lie as the Trickster. All other players form the Jury to interrogate them and vote on their phones!

👑 Running the Game as Host

As the Host, you control the main screen and guide the party through each thrilling round.

🎭 You Are The Trickster (The Bluffer)

Your Mission: You have been secretly commanded to LIE. Fabricate a fake story on the spot and fool the Jury!

🗣️ You Are Telling The Truth

Your Mission: Tell a 100% TRUE personal story from your real life about your assigned topic.

⚖️ You Are The Jury

Your Mission: Interrogate the Speaker, spot contradictions, and cast your vote on your phone!

🏆 Scoring Matrix

Trickster wins +20 PTS if fooled. Jurors or Truth Tellers win +10 PTS on correct verdicts.
